en: "Two BALAB teams swept the top places at the <strong>Shipbuilding &amp; Marine Public Data and Big Data Competition</strong>, part of Busan's national innovation cluster initiative, hosted by the <strong>Korea Marine Equipment Research Institute (KOMERI)</strong> and <strong>Busan Techno Park</strong> at the Westin Josun Busan on December 20. Team <strong>MGA</strong> — Mingyu Park, Gian Antariksa, and Agung Nugraha — took the <strong>Grand Prize (대상)</strong> for a data-driven wave-height forecasting and early-warning service for safe ship operations. Team <strong>Baves</strong> — Melia Putri Handayani, Hyeonji Jeong, Sieun Kim, and Duguma Yeshitla — took the <strong>Excellence Award (최우수상)</strong> for explainable fuel-consumption prediction of bulk carriers using SHAP."
